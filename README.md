# Power BI — Tutedude Certification Tasks

This is where I'm keeping the 20 tasks from my **Power BI** certification with **Tutedude**. Each folder is a different task, written up in my own words — what I actually built, what I found in the data, and what clicked for me along the way, rather than just a checklist of completed steps.

The tasks build up in a pretty natural arc: starting from the basics of the tool, into proper data modeling with fact/dimension tables, then a long stretch of DAX (from simple aggregations through to time intelligence and iterator functions), and finally a series of dashboard-building tasks that bring everything together into something genuinely interactive.

> 🎓 My capstone project — a full end-to-end Power BI dashboard — lives in its own separate repo, since it's meant to stand on its own as a portfolio piece rather than sit buried as task #21 here.
